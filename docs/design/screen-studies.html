Three screens built from the same stationery as the letters: paper cards, washi tape in each persona's ink and pattern, and die-cut stickers. Portraits are ink-wash stand-ins for NovelAI art, and fonts are still placeholders.

Identity carries through

A persona's ink and tape pattern appear on its list card, its polaroid, its page and its letters, so you can tell who's who at a glance. Your own ink and tape do the same for your notes and the active tab.

Unread is a sticker

The new-letter mark is a die-cut 新 seal in the persona's ink, using the same outline setting as every other sticker.

Memory, at a glance

Her page shows a few memories as slips: core ones taped down, episodes dated, her own reflections in italic. The full book opens from there.

Colours are madoromi by samhain (MPL-2.0), measured from Pilot Iroshizuku inks. Fonts are placeholders: Cormorant Garamond, Literata, LINE Seed JP and Yuji Syuku. Portraits and sticker faces stand in for NovelAI art.
