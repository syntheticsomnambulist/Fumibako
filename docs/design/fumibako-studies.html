Little fumibakos

Each correspondent's letters in a box of their own, drawn the way the writing desk is: flat shapes in their ink mixed into the paper, thin lines for the cord and seals, and the app's soft shadow. Each persona picks their box, the way they pick their unread mark: a finish, and what's on the lid. The open letter's box has its lid off and leaning behind it, the cord undone, and inside, on a lining in their ink, their letters. Click a box to open or shut it, and try other boxes in the cards below.

Each picks their own選

A card on each persona's page, beside their unread mark: the box shut and open, a finish, and what's on the lid. A persona could pick for themselves too, the way they choose their mood.

In the letters list文

With each persona's own box, the list reads best with the words beside the box: their name, mood and latest lines on the page, the box as an object beside them. The paper label on the box (the earlier drawing, now flat) holds the words itself, so it suits every box alike.

Each box as its persona chose it; Aria's is open.

The words on the box

A paper label on every lid, with the latest lines; the finish is theirs.

Only the boxes

A shelf, three to a row, with the name under each box that doesn't carry it.

On the phone携

On the phone, the letters list fills the screen and a letter opens as a page of its own, in place of the list, so the list never has a box open in it. The choice is whether the phone's list becomes boxes too, every one shut, or keeps today's paper cards, with boxes only where there's room for the list beside an open letter (a computer, a tablet, a phone on its side).

Today's cards

As the phone has them now: each card held by its persona's washi tape.

Boxes, all shut

The same list as boxes. Opening a letter here takes you to it, so no box stays open.

The lining in every ink墨

All fourteen of madoromi's inks, each box open and emptied: the floor at 40% of the ink in the paper, the walls deeper (55% at the sides, 70% at the back), the lacquer outside at 70% with its front edge at 85%. Night inks at night.

Drawn like the desk

Flat shapes in tints of the ink, one deeper tint for the front edge, thin lines only for the cord, the bow and seals, no gradients, and the app's soft shadow. The open box is drawn as the desk's trays are: back wall, side walls, floor and front lip.

Their seal

The seal on the lid is the persona's signature seal (one of the persona marks agreed for the planner), here their ink's kanji. Their unread mark still goes on the lid's corner when a letter's new.

Engraved

The name cut into the lid: the letters in the box's deeper tint with a thin lighter line under each, where the cut catches the light. On washi it reads as pressed into the paper.

Opening one

In the app the bow could undo and the lid lift back as the letter opens beside the list, in about a quarter of a second, and shut again when another opens.

To choose

  1. Chosen: each persona picks their own box; the night's inks at night.
  2. The finishes and lids: keep all three finishes (lacquer, washi, paulownia) and all four lids (name plate, seal, engraved name, their kanji), or fewer?
  3. The list: the words beside the box, the words on the box (a paper label on every lid), or only the boxes.
  4. On the phone: boxes there too, all shut, or today's paper cards.

Colours are madoromi by samhain (MPL-2.0), measured from Pilot Iroshizuku inks. Faces from the v0.5 font audit: Zen Old Mincho, Literata, Zen Kaku Gothic New, Yuji Syuku and Yomogi. Portraits stand in for NovelAI art, and the people and letters are examples.
