Little fumibakos

Each correspondent's letters in a box of their own, drawn the way the writing desk is: flat shapes in their ink mixed into the paper, thin lines only for details, and the app's soft shadow. Each box is short and chubby: a lid, a deeper band for the lid's edge, the box below, and a cord with a bow. Opening one undoes the bow, tips the lid back and lifts their letter; on the phone it then brings you to the letter. Personas choose their own box. Click any box.

On the phone携

The list as shut boxes, each with its latest lines beside it. Tap a box: it opens, then the letter slides in. Back closes the letter, and the box shuts again behind it.

Beside an open letter文

In a wide window the open letter's box stays open while you read it beside the list; opening another shuts the first.

Their own box選

A card on each persona's page. They choose their box themselves, the finish and what's on the lid, and can change it when they like; you can choose for them instead.

Every finish, every lid蓋

Three finishes and five lids, Aria's ink, at half again the size: her name on a plate, her seal, her name cut in, her name gilded, and her ink's kanji. Click one to open it.

The lining in every ink墨

All fourteen inks, each box open: the floor at 40% of the ink in the paper, the side walls at 55%, the back wall at 70%.

A box at a glance

Three shapes say "box": the lid's top, a deeper band for its edge, and the box under it, a little narrower. The cord runs over all three, and the bow sits on the lid.

Opening

About half a second: the bow undoes, the lid tips back on its far edge, their letter lifts a little inside. On the phone the letter's page follows; closing it shuts the box behind you.

Gilded

Their name cut in and filled with gold: the gold is the yamabuki ink, lightened, with a deeper line under each letter. At night it's the night's yamabuki, so it glows a little.

The strip

The name, mood, time and latest lines on a strip of paper with a torn end, tucked under the box's side; the tag is the same words on a tag tied to the box with a thread.

Decided, and to choose

  1. Decided: each persona chooses their own box, the night's inks at night, shut boxes on the phone that open before the letter comes in.
  2. The words: on a paper strip, or on a tag tied to the box?
  3. The lids: keep all five (name, seal, engraved, gilded, kanji) and all three finishes, or fewer?
  4. The drawing: is this cute and clear enough to build, or another pass first?
