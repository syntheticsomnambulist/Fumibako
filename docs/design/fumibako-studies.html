Five boxes箱

Aria's box is open; Juno's has an unread letter (her seal on the lid); Kestrel's is shut. Each piece is the letters list's width on a phone (360).

Only the name名

Without the latest lines, a box needs only a name slip, so boxes can be slimmer, or sit two to a row like boxes on a shelf. Open, the slip goes with the lid, and inside are their letters, folded, the top one with their name. Pick the box for these and for the inks below.

The lining in every ink墨

All fourteen of madoromi's inks, each box open. The lining follows the ink's own ramp, as madoromi measures each ink from pooled to wash: deepest in the corners, paler towards the middle.

Boxes are objects

Stickers and polaroid frames keep the season's day inks at night, since they're things on the desk rather than ink on the page. The boxes do too, by default; the switch above shows them in the night's inks instead.

Opening one

In the app, opening a letter could undo the bow and lift the lid back in a quarter of a second; closing it, the reverse. On the phone, with no letter beside the list, every box stays shut.

The unread seal

Each persona's unread mark (a seal, an emoji or one of their stickers) goes on the lid, where it is on the card today. It comes off when the box opens.

Fewer things on the lid

With only the name, the list shows less at a glance (no time, no lines) but reads as a row of boxes. The time could stay on the slip; the seal still says there's something new.

To choose

  1. The box: lacquer in their ink, black lacquer with their ink inside, paulownia, covered in their washi, or lacquer with their kanji in gold (maki-e). Or a mix: a box per persona, chosen in their settings like their unread mark.
  2. On the lid: the name and their latest lines, or only the name; and if only the name, a list or a shelf.
  3. At night: day inks, as stickers keep, or the night's.
  4. On the phone: boxes there too (always shut), or the phone's cards as they are.
