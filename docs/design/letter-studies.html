Three sources

Emoji to start. Each persona's own pack: a sticker sheet NovelAI draws from their appearance tags, which only they and you use in their chats. A shared library you upload to, usable with every persona, which also holds stamps like 花丸 and ink strokes.

Anywhere, any size

Place a sticker anywhere on a letter, a note or your draft. Move it, resize it, rotate it, peel it off. By default each of you can only remove your own; two settings let you remove hers, or let her remove yours. The outline is a hard die-cut edge, white by default, recoloured in settings. Each sticker keeps who placed it, where, its size, angle and time.

Personas use them freely

Stickers have :names:, which is how a persona chooses one: the model reads names, not pictures. A persona can stick one on your notes or its own letters whenever it likes, through a tool, and write custom emoji inline in its text.

They see stickers, moods are their own

Stickers you place reach the persona as short notes on its next turn, shown under the phone. It reacts however it wants and picks its own mood word; stickers never set it. They're drawn over the letter, so the text and the saved history never change.

Auto uses today's season and your device's light or dark setting; in the app it would follow the clock. Stickers are printed in each season's day inks. Colours are madoromi by samhain (MPL-2.0), measured from Pilot Iroshizuku inks. Type: Cormorant Garamond, Literata, LINE Seed JP and Yuji Syuku. Portraits, Aria's sticker faces and the generated image are placeholders for NovelAI art.
