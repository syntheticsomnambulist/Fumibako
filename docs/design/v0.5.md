# v0.5: import and export, the stationery update, and quality of life

Started 2026-11-06 in the design session, while the build session works on v0.4. Quoted app text
follows the app's American spelling.

## What's in it (agreed)

The user's list, and only that:

1. **In a letter:** the context slip ("What she had in mind") beside the thoughts, each with an
   icon of its own; thinking lines the persona chooses; a note when a persona's model changes.
2. **Searching past letters,** for you and, as a tool, for personas.
3. **Import and export:** SillyTavern cards in and out, lore memories and unfadeable memories (see
   *Import and export* in [`ideas.md`](ideas.md)), and backups. The export page offers both a
   persona on its own and everything; the default is the persona with its memories and pictures.
4. **The stationery update:** the new tape patterns (nine, including hearts), traditional month
   names on the memory book's envelopes, and the like. v0.4 already brings the 12-hour clock,
   "Seasons follow" and persona marks.
5. **A font audit, from scratch:** the current faces are placeholders. With the design space much
   clearer now, every face is chosen on purpose and they're aligned with each other.

Order of design: in a letter, then search, then import and export, then the stationery update and
fonts (last, so the other mockups aren't redrawn twice).

## Today

- **A letter** is a paper card with the persona's washi strip along its top; a row with its
  portrait, name, mood word and time; then the letter, any frames, and P.S. lines for memories it
  kept.
- **"Show thoughts"** is a text toggle under that row, opening the model's reasoning in italics.
- **While it writes,** a line in italics says "writing…" or what tool it's using ("Searching
  memories").
- **Only the memory book has search.**
- **Nothing tells a persona its model changed,** and there's no import or export.

## In a letter

### Two tabs at the top (agreed)

- **The thoughts and the context slip are two paper tabs** sticking up from behind the letter's
  top edge, at the right (the washi strip keeps the left). **Icons only, no labels:** an index card
  with a paperclip for **What she had in mind**, a scribbled scrap for **Her thoughts**. Each tab is
  cut from its slip's paper. Screen readers get the names, and a long press shows them as a
  tooltip.
- **Tapping a tab slides its slip out at the top of the letter,** under the row with her name and
  before the letter itself; tapping again tucks it away. The open tab stands a little taller, its
  icon in her ink. Both can be out at once, in the order of the turn: the index card (what she
  had), her thoughts (what she made of it), then the letter (what she wrote).
- **Both slips are sheets of their own** (agreed: the thoughts needed a container). **Her
  thoughts** (only when the model gave any): a scrap of squared paper, pale yamabuki, with a torn
  top edge and a hard shadow; the reasoning in the postscript face.
- **What she had in mind:** an index card (a red rule at the top, faint ruled lines) with what the
  turn's context block held, in sections: **The time** ("Friday 6 November, 16:04, five hours after
  your last note"), **Your planner** (its line, from v0.4), **Memories that came up** (each with its
  feeling stamp), **Stickers** (the notes about stickers placed or peeled), **What she read** (the
  descriptions of attachments), **Letters she looked up** (see *Searching letters*), and at the
  foot, **this turn's tokens** as the provider reported them (read and written). From v0.6, **her
  desk** (the desk line, and the note she took the letter up with). Nothing is fetched to show it:
  the context block is already stored on the message.
- **Letters written at the desk** (v0.6) show the desk turn's context instead, the same way.

### Thinking lines

- **While she writes,** the italic line says one of her own lines, with her name: "Aria is chewing
  her pen", changing every few seconds, and a line of her own for each tool ("Aria is leafing
  through her memory book" for searching memories, "rereading your old letters" for searching
  letters, "pressing a memory between the pages" for keeping one, "reading the page you sent" for
  opening a link, "sketching in the margin" for a picture). A small ellipsis writes itself after
  it.
- **The letters list** shows the same line on her card while she writes.
- **Where they come from:** her page gets a **While she writes** card: a few thinking lines and
  one per tool, which you can edit, and **Let Aria write her own**, a short turn (under 1,000
  tokens) in which she writes them in her voice. New personas start with plain ones ("Aria is
  thinking", "Aria is looking through her memories").
- **She rewrites her own when she reflects** (agreed: no standing tool). The heartbeat's
  reflection shows her current lines, and she may change any of hers, **at most once a week**
  (agreed), so they stay recognisable. Proposed: through a small tool offered only in that private
  moment and never in letters. "While you were away" says so ("Aria rewrote two of her writing
  lines").
- **Lines you write or edit stay yours:** she leaves them alone, and her page marks them "yours".

### When her model changes

- **She's told** in the first turn after the change, in the context block (not the system
  prompt): "Your model changed: you were Claude Sonnet 5.5 until now and are Claude Opus 5.5 from
  here. Your memories and letters are the same." She can mention it or not.
- **You see it** in the thread, at the point of the change, as a small notice like a
  change-of-address card: a 改 stamp and "From here, Aria writes with Claude Opus 5.5 (was Claude
  Sonnet 5.5)".
- **A tick on her model setting,** "Tell her when her model changes", on by default. Turned off,
  she isn't told, but the notice still shows in your thread: the thread is your record.
- **Her model row** on her page says since when ("Since today, 15:40 · was Claude Sonnet 5.5").

### Her page: While she writes

- **Thinking** lines, each shown as "Aria is" plus her line in her ink, with a remove button, and
  **+ Add a line**.
- **When she…**, one editable line per tool: looks through her memories, looks through old
  letters, keeps a memory, opens a link, draws a picture, picks a sticker.
- **Let Aria write her own** (a short turn, under 1,000 tokens) fills both with lines in her voice.
  Afterwards the button reads **Ask her for new ones**, and the note says when she wrote them.
  Lines marked "yours" stay as they are.

### Shadows and the page

- **Soft shadows everywhere** (agreed, after a side-by-side with hard ones): the app's own, a 2dp
  elevation under cards (`paperCard`), a little more under frames, flat fields and buttons. The
  desk keeps them too, replacing the hard offset its mockups were drawn with (see
  [`activities.md`](activities.md)). The v0.5 mockups are redrawn with them.
- **No paper texture, for now** (agreed). Two were tried on the page under the same pieces: a
  fine grain read as a dimming layer, and washi fibres looked like cat hair. The page stays one
  flat `hyoshi`; a texture could come back later, coarser than the grain.

## Searching letters (proposed)

### For you

- **Where:** a loupe beside the Letters title opens **Search letters**. In a thread, ⋯ ›
  **Search these letters** opens it for that persona.
- **What it searches:** her letters, your notes, and what's in pictures (captions, and the
  descriptions the app keeps of drawings and photos). Not her thoughts or the context slips.
- **The page:** an index-card search field ("A word, a name, a place"); filters for whose letters
  (Everyone, or one persona) and what kind (Everything, Their letters, Your notes, Pictures); recent
  searches while the field is empty.
- **Results,** newest first: a slip for each letter with the writer's tape, who and when ("You to
  Aria, Wed 4 Nov"), and the line around the word, the word marked like a highlighter in the
  writer's ink. A picture shows a small polaroid.
- **Tapping a result** opens the thread at that letter. A strip under the header says "“cat” · 3 of
  3 in your letters with Aria", with older and newer arrows and **Done**. Every match in the thread
  is marked, and the current one is darker, with a ring round its letter or picture.
- **Words, not meaning,** to start: a word index over the letters (SQLite's FTS4, which Android
  has), matching the start of words so "cat" finds "cats". Searching by meaning would need an
  embedding for every letter; later, if it's wanted.

### For personas: `letter_search`

- **A tool beside `memory_search`:** `query` (words), `from` (`you`, `me` or `either`) and `limit`
  (default 5, at most 10). It returns passages, a few sentences around each match, with who wrote
  them and when; letters with more of the words come first, then newer ones.
- **Only her own letters with you** (whichever of your personas wrote them). Never another
  persona's letters; letters to pen pals are for v0.6 to decide.
- **Its thinking line:** "Aria is looking through old letters" (plain), or her own.
- **In her letter:** a P.S. line, "Looked back at: your note of Mon 12 Oct", naming only the top
  result (agreed) and opening the thread there. The index card's **Letters she looked up** shows what she searched
  for and every passage that came back, each opening its letter.
- **A switch** beside Memory tools: "Letter search: personas look through your past letters with
  them". On by default.

## Import and export (proposed)

Builds on *Import and export* in [`ideas.md`](ideas.md). Agreed so far: the export page offers
one persona and everything, and its default is one persona with her memories and pictures; only
you mark a memory "Won't fade"; an Everything backup replaces what's on the phone; backups can be
locked with a password that encrypts them. The rest is proposed.

### Where

- **Importing:** **Import a card…** beside **New persona** (in the gallery and in "Write to…");
  opening or sharing a card file into the app; and Settings › Import and export › **Open a
  file…**, which takes cards and backups alike and tells them apart by what's inside.
- **A lorebook on its own** (SillyTavern's World Info JSON) goes into a persona you already have,
  from her memory book: ⋯ › **Add lore from a lorebook…**, with the same lore preview.
- **Exporting:** her page › ⋯ › **Export Aria…**, or Settings › Import and export › **Export**.

### Importing a card

- **It reads** Character Card V2 and V3: PNG (the `chara` and `ccv3` chunks; V3 wins when there
  are both), JSON, and `.charx`.
- **The page** shows her portrait, name, creator, version and tags, then **From the card's
  creator** (`creator_notes`): shown to you, never sent to her.
- **What comes in,** with ticks:
  - **Who she is** (description, personality, scenario): always.
  - **Greetings** (on): `first_mes` and the `alternate_greetings`, to pick from when you start a
    letter.
  - **Example dialogue** (on).
  - **Lore** (on) becomes lore memories, with **Won't fade** (on). Entries the card switched off
    are left out and counted; `constant` entries become always in mind; `keys` become the memory's
    recall words. **See them** shows the slips.
  - **Mood portraits** (on): V3 `emotion` assets.
  - **The card's own prompts,** off, each readable first: `system_prompt` would replace
    Fumibako's role section (`systemPromptOverride`), and `post_history_instructions` would go at
    the end of each turn's context block.
- **Her ink** is suggested from the portrait (the nearest of the nine), with the tape that goes
  with it; both change later on her page.
- **What it costs:** "Every letter carries about 2,200 tokens of her card", updating with the
  ticks.
- **Kept as it came:** the rest of the card (fields Fumibako doesn't use, `extensions`, lorebook
  settings), so exporting her gives it back. `{{char}}` and `{{user}}` work as in SillyTavern.
- **A card Fumibako made** also brings back her ink, tape, pronouns, picture prompts and writing
  lines, from `extensions.fumibako`. Other cards don't carry pronouns: she starts without, as a
  new persona does.

### Lore memories and "Won't fade"

- **Lore is a new kind of memory.** In her memory book it has a "lore" label where a feeling
  stamp would be (lore isn't felt) and shows its recall words. It's never offered back to revisit
  and doesn't count towards "matters deeply".
- **Won't fade** (a small 永 seal on the slip): the memory never weakens or goes dormant, but is
  still recalled only when it's relevant. Lore comes in with it on, and any memory can have it.
- **Only you set it** (agreed), from a memory's card. Not the persona: she has "matters deeply"
  for what she holds on to.

### Exporting

- **Two tabs:** **One persona** (the default) and **Everything**.
- **One persona, as a backup** (the default): a `.fumibako` file with her (page, look, stickers,
  writing lines, picture prompts), **her memories** (on, with their keepsakes) and **her
  pictures** (on). **Your letters with her** are off.
- **One persona, as a character card:** her portrait as a PNG with the card inside (V3, with a V2
  copy for older apps). Her lore goes as the lorebook (on). Her other memories can go too (off:
  other apps can't feel them, so they go as plain facts). Fumibako's own fields go under
  `extensions.fumibako`.
- **Everything:** all personas and yours, memories, pictures, letters, the planner, and settings
  with models. All on.
- **Never:** API keys. From v0.6, memories in confidence are left out unless ticked.
- **Lock it with a password** (agreed; off by default; backups only, never cards): the password
  twice, and a line in `beni` saying a forgotten password can't be recovered. The file is
  encrypted with it, and the file's name gets "· locked".
- **The page** gives the file's name and size, and says that before 1.0 a backup opens only in
  the version that made it. **Save the file…** opens Android's save dialog; **Share…** the share
  sheet.

### Opening a backup

- **The page** shows the file (when it was made, by which version, its size) and what's in it.
- **If she's already here:** **Keep both** (the default: the backup's Aria comes in beside her as
  "Aria (6 Nov)", with a thread of her own) or **Replace her** (her page, memories and pictures
  become the backup's; your letters with her stay in the same thread; what she kept since is
  lost; the button turns `beni`).
- **Her model** comes without its key. If this phone has no key for that provider, the page says
  so with **Add a key**; she comes in either way and writes once there's one.
- **A locked backup** asks for its password first, and shows nothing of what's inside until it's
  unlocked.
- **An Everything backup replaces all of Fumibako on the phone** (agreed: adding only what's
  missing leaves too many gaps for problems). The page says what goes ("Aria, Juno and Kit, 2,260
  letters, and all that's happened since 6 Nov. Kit isn't in the backup, so Kit goes."), offers
  **Export everything first**, and the button is **Replace everything** in `beni`. The phone's
  keys stay, so everyone can write straight away.
- **From another version,** before 1.0: the page says which version made it and doesn't open it.
  From 1.0 older backups open, when Room migrations start.
- **The file:** a zip holding a manifest (format version, app version, when, what's inside), the
  records as JSON, and the pictures as files. A persona who came from a card carries the original
  card inside too.
- **A locked file** (proposed): a short plain header (that it's a locked Fumibako backup, the
  format and app versions, the salt and key-derivation settings), then the whole zip encrypted
  with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from the password with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (built into
  Android; a random salt, about 600,000 iterations). The header has the version so a backup from
  another version is refused before asking for the password; nothing else is readable.

## Mockups

- **In a letter:** a thread with your note, the model-change notice and Aria's letter; the two

- **Soft shadows, plain and on paper:** the same pieces (a letters list card, a note, a letter, a
  polaroid, a memory slip, a field and a button) on today's plain page and on a textured one, with
  the fine grain and the washi fibres that were tried and set aside.
- **Search letters:** type a word (try "rain", "portfolio" or "kinako"); the filters work.
- **Found in the letters:** the thread opened from a result; step through the matches, press Done,
  then ⋯ › Search these letters.
- **She looks it up:** Aria answers from an old letter; the index card shows what she found, and
  the P.S. links back.
- **Import a card:** a SillyTavern card for "Mira"; tick and untick (the token count follows),
  see the lore slips, read the card's system prompt, pick her ink.
- **Export:** one persona or everything; a backup or a character card; the file's size follows the
  ticks; lock a backup with a password.
- **Open a backup:** when she's already here, keep both or replace her. The chips at the foot
  switch to a locked backup (unlock it), an Everything backup (what it replaces), and one from 0.4
  (refused).
